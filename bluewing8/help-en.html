BlueWing 8 Help

About This App

BlueWing 8 is a community app for dialogue and peace, built on public information such as Wikipedia. It is a free service run by an individual, Yukio Okazaki.

How to Use

  • News: You will see the latest articles. Each time you open the app, new content arrives from Wikimedia.
  • Language switch: You can choose your language from the language field at the top of News. If a real article exists in that language on Wikipedia, we will show it. If not, you can read a translation from "Read with Google Translate."
  • Music: When you press the play button on a card, free music from Wikimedia Commons will play.
  • Videos and streaming music: You can watch and listen directly through YouTube's official player.
  • Search: You can search for articles by entering a word.

    • Home Screen Icon

    If you tap "Add to Home Screen" from your browser menu, a blue wing icon will be placed on your home screen. Next time, you can open the app from that icon.

    About Data

    The current public version is Phase 1, and posts and members are samples. What you type will disappear when you close the page. Real accounts and posts will be prepared in the next phase.

    Does it cost money?

    No. It is free.

    If you need help

    Please contact the operator, Yukio Okazaki, through his public contact information, such as the Facebook account "Yukio Okazaki." Requests for deletion are also accepted there.
