BlueWing8 Listing Policy

Operator: Yukio Okazaki (individual)

Established: November 28, 2026

1. About this policy

The "Institutions & Media" corner and "From our community" shelf of BlueWing8 introduce official videos, Reel videos and articles from universities, research institutions, media and individuals. This policy sets out the rules for listing applications and listings. Please read it before applying.

2. How listings appear

  • Videos are introduced through official embeds such as YouTube. Video files themselves are never hosted on this service.
  • Reel videos and articles are introduced as guide cards with a title, a short introduction and a link. Full texts are never reprinted.
  • Listings are free of charge. No payment is made or requested for a listing.

    • 3. Review points

    Applications are reviewed by the operator on the following points before listing.

  • The content is published by the applicant themselves or is the institution's official channel (names of well-known persons and institutions are checked with particular care)
  • The link opens correctly
  • Rights are handled within the scope of embedding and linking
  • The content is not inappropriate (see 4. below)

    • 4. What we do not list

  • Content that uses others' works, photos or videos without the rights holder's permission
  • Impersonation or false information
  • Content that harms others, defamation, or discriminatory expression
  • Content containing others' personal information (addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, etc.)
  • Content that violates laws or public order and morals
  • Content whose sole purpose is advertising

    • 5. Removal requests

    If the applicant or a rights holder asks for a listing to be removed, we will check the request and act promptly. Please contact us through the "Contact / Deletion Request" window inside the app. Requests from rights holders are handled appropriately under applicable law.

    6. Personal information

    Email addresses and contact names provided in applications are used only for review and communication, and are never published. See the Privacy Policy for details.

    7. Disclaimer

    The content of linked and embedded pages belongs to their respective publishers. This service does not guarantee the accuracy or legality of linked content.

    8. Revisions

    Revisions to this policy will be announced on this service.
