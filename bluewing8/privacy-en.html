BlueWing 8 Privacy Policy

Operator: Yukio Okazaki (individual)

Enacted: November 26, 2026 / Revised: November 28, 2026

1. Basic Policy

BlueWing 8 (hereinafter "this Service") respects your privacy and handles personal information only to the minimum extent necessary and in an appropriate manner.

2. About the Current Public Version

The current public version handles real account registrations, posts and comments. The information handled, and the purposes, are described in 4. and 5. below.

3. Use of External Services

This Service uses the systems of the following external services. When you use them, their respective privacy policies apply.

  • Public information and APIs from the Wikimedia Foundation (Wikipedia, Wikinews, Wikimedia Commons)
  • Official embedded player from YouTube (Google)
  • GitHub Pages (as a public hosting place)

    • 4. Information Handled for Accounts and Posts

    We handle the following information to the extent necessary for operating the service.

  • Registration information (display name, email address)
  • Text and photos you post
  • Records related to your use of the service

    • We will not use this information for any purpose other than providing the service and operating it safely. Except with your consent or as required by law, we will not provide it to any third party.

    5. Information Handled for Listing Applications

    Through the listing application form, we handle the following information.

  • Institution name or your name, and the contact person's name
  • Email address
  • The URL of the video, Reel or article you wish to list, and your message
  • Titles, images and similar information automatically retrieved from that URL

    • These are used only for reviewing the listing and contacting the applicant. Email addresses and contact names are never published. If a listing is approved, only the institution name or your name, and the title and URL of the content, are published. On the applicant's request, we will delete the application information.

    6. Where Data Is Stored and How It Is Managed

    User data is stored in the database of a professional cloud service (Cloudflare). The operator does not store users' personal data itself on personal devices such as a smartphone. The keys for accessing the data are kept in secure storage.

    Day-to-day management work (reviewing applications, responding to deletion requests, and similar tasks) is carried out by an administrator entrusted by the operator (Muse), under the responsibility of the operator, Yukio Okazaki. Even where storage and day-to-day work are entrusted, responsibility for the handling of personal information remains with the operator.

    7. Deletion and Inquiries

    For deletion of your own information or inquiries about this policy, please contact us through the "Contact / Deletion Request" window inside the app. We will check the details and respond appropriately.

    8. Revisions

    If we revise this policy, we will inform you on this Service.
